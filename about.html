About {{ site.threevoices.title }}

{{ site.threevoices.title }} records how voice AI is applied in autism care, agriculture and industrial field operations, with a step‑by‑step account of how the systems work and how to prototype similar tools. Every issue features verified, distinct use cases across the three domains.

In scope

Voice-AI systems where speech input or spoken output is central, used in autism care (Medical · Autism), agriculture, or industrial and field operations. Text-first tools with optional voice features are out of scope.

What counts as a source

News outlets, peer‑reviewed journals, universities, government bodies, research institutes, standards organisations and non‑profits.

What is left out

Vendor case studies, AI‑company blog posts, consulting reports and social‑media testimonials, even when they tell a good story.

The fine print

  • Each issue selects distinct voice-AI use cases across autism care, agriculture and industrial operations, based on strong sourcing and relevance that month.
  • Many projects are pilots. Where results are early, self‑reported or from one small study, the case says so in a “Read with care” note.
  • Health tools here are research or pilot systems, not replacements for clinicians. Industrial prototypes require operator verification and must never bypass machine or site safety controls.
