About {{ site.threevoices.title }}

{{ site.threevoices.title }} records how voice AI is applied in autism care, agriculture and space technologies, with a step‑by‑step account of how the systems work and how to prototype similar tools. Starting with the next issue, each domain will have three cases per month.

In scope

Voice-AI systems where speech input or spoken output is central, used in autism care (Medical · Autism), agriculture, or space technologies. Text-first tools with optional voice features are out of scope.

What counts as a source

News outlets, peer‑reviewed journals, universities, space agencies and other public bodies, research institutes and non‑profits.

What is left out

Vendor case studies, AI‑company blog posts, consulting reports and social‑media testimonials, even when they tell a good story.

The fine print

  • Starting with the next issue, each issue covers three voice-AI use cases per domain, selected for strong sourcing and relevance that month.
  • Many projects are pilots. Where results are early, self‑reported or from one small study, the case says so in a “Read with care” note.
  • Health tools here, for autism or astronaut care, are research or pilot systems, not replacements for clinicians.
